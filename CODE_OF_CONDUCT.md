# Code of conduct

Helpers is built for, and with, people who find reading and writing hard work. Everyone here should feel safe to ask, to suggest, and to get things wrong.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient.
- Assume good intent.
- Never mock spelling, grammar or the way someone writes. Read for what they mean.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Make room for people who are new, slower to write, or using tools to help them.

## What isn't OK

- Insults, put-downs, or jokes about anyone's abilities, disability, identity or background.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything you wouldn't say to someone's face at work.

## If something goes wrong

Tell a maintainer. Mention @drhumphrey in a comment, or use the contact details on their GitHub profile if it needs to be private. We'll listen, keep it confidential, and act: from a quiet word to removing someone from the project.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
